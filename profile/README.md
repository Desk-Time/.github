## Understanding Desk Time Core Components

Desk Time is not just a simple timer. It includes several powerful features:

- **Automatic activity tracking** — Desk Time detects which application or window is active and records your **Desk Time** accordingly.
- **Idle detection** — When you step away from your computer, Desk Time pauses tracking until you return to your **Desk Time** session.
- **Category system** — Group apps into Work, Communication, Entertainment, or create custom categories for better **Desk Time** analysis.
- **Daily and weekly reports** — See your **Desk Time** trends over time. Identify your most productive hours each day.
- **Local database** — All your **Desk Time** history stays on your machine. No cloud uploads, no third-party access.
- **Portable mode** — Run Desk Time from a USB drive. Take your **Desk Time** tracking anywhere.
- **Goal setting** — Define a target **Desk Time** for focused work each day and receive gentle reminders.

All these components work together to give you complete control over your **Desk Time** without compromising your privacy.
